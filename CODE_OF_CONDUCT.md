# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ComptaRAG is a small, open project, and we want it to be a welcoming space for anyone who wants to use it, learn from it, or contribute to it regardless of experience level, background, nationality, language, gender, religion, or any other personal characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are about ideas, not people critique the code or the proposal, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with contributors who aren't native English (or French) speakers; clarity matters more than polish.
- Give constructive feedback: explain the "why" behind a suggestion, not just the "what."
- Respect maintainers' and contributors' time this is largely a volunteer effort.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes, language, or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information (e.g. contact details) without explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions, trolling, or sustained disruptive behavior.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or community setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages) and in any other setting when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the maintainer at **elhammemi001@gmail.com**. Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible. Please include enough detail (links, screenshots, context) for the maintainer to look into it.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct, and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that don't align with it, or temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
